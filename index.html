Q6. Explain the audio signal flow in a conference room.

A microphone captures sound. The signal may pass through a digital signal processor for gain control, routing and echo cancellation, then into the conferencing system and out to remote participants. Incoming audio follows the return path through the processor or room system to the speakers. Understanding that path helps isolate faults.

⚠️ Schéma conceptuel : mic → DSP → conférence → distant ; retour → speakers.

Q7. What is a DSP?

A digital signal processor processes audio signals. It can handle mixing, equalisation, gain, routing, noise control and acoustic echo cancellation, depending on the configuration.

⚠️ Q-SYS utilise notamment ce type de traitement.

Q8. What is AEC?

Acoustic Echo Cancellation reduces the echo created when a microphone picks up sound from the room speakers and sends it back to the far end. I would first check for duplicate audio devices and then inspect the room audio path and AEC configuration through the approved process.

⚠️ AEC ≠ simple mute ; c’est un traitement audio.

Q9. What is EDID?

Extended Display Identification Data is information a display provides to a source about supported resolutions and display capabilities. A failed HDMI or EDID negotiation can contribute to a no-signal problem.

⚠️ Tester source, câble, port, résolution et adaptateur.

Q10. What is HDCP?

High-bandwidth Digital Content Protection is a content-protection system used over digital audiovisual connections. Compatibility or handshake issues can prevent protected content from displaying. I would not try to bypass protection.

⚠️ Différent d’EDID.

Q11. HDMI, DisplayPort, USB-C and USB: what is the difference?

HDMI and DisplayPort carry digital video and audio. USB can connect cameras, microphones and other peripherals. USB-C is a connector that may carry USB data, power and, on compatible devices, video through alternate modes. The connector alone does not guarantee every capability.

⚠️ Attention aux adaptateurs et docks.

Q12. What is AV over IP?

AV over IP transports audiovisual signals or control information over an IP network. Depending on the system, troubleshooting may involve addressing, switch ports, VLANs, multicast traffic, latency and quality of service. I would check the vendor design and the network team’s procedures.

⚠️ Ne pas présumer que toutes les salles Evercore utilisent multicast.

Q13. What is PoE?

Power over Ethernet supplies electrical power and network connectivity over Ethernet cabling to compatible devices. If a network-connected controller or camera is offline, I would check the switch port and power budget as well as the device.

⚠️ PoE dépend du matériel.

Q14. Why might AV devices use a separate VLAN?

A dedicated VLAN can help segment AV devices and apply appropriate access-control and traffic policies. Quality of service can also be configured where required. Segmentation alone does not guarantee performance or security.

⚠️ Nuance importante : VLAN ≠ QoS automatique.

Compléments techniques AV — définitions à comprendre

  • Signal flow — Trajet du signal : microphone → traitement / DSP → système de conférence → distant ; retour vers haut-parleurs. Repérer l'étape où le signal disparaît.
  • AEC — Acoustic Echo Cancellation : limite le retour de la voix distante captée par les micros locaux ; ne se confond pas avec la suppression du larsen.
  • Gain / volume — Le gain règle le niveau d'entrée, le volume le niveau d'écoute ; un gain trop élevé peut saturer (clipping).
  • EDID — Informations communiquées par un écran à la source (résolutions, fréquences, audio) ; un problème de négociation peut provoquer un écran noir.
  • HDCP — Protection de certains contenus numériques ; incompatibilités possibles dans une chaîne HDMI.
  • USB-C — Connecteur multifonction ; la vidéo dépend du matériel et des modes pris en charge, elle n'est pas garantie sur tous les ports.
  • PoE — Alimentation électrique par Ethernet pour certains périphériques compatibles ; vérifier alimentation et budget du commutateur.
  • AV over IP — Transport/commande de flux AV via réseau ; selon le produit, vérifier IP, VLAN, multicast, QoS, latence et pertes.
  • BYOD — Bring Your Own Device : utiliser son ordinateur pour rejoindre une réunion avec les périphériques de la salle, lorsque la configuration l'autorise.
  • Room resource account — Compte ou ressource calendrier associé à une salle ; vérifier invitations, synchronisation et droits selon la plateforme.

Q5. What is the difference between audio gain and volume?

Gain adjusts the level of a signal at a processing stage, while volume usually refers to the listening level at the output. Excessive gain can cause distortion or feedback. I would not change DSP gain settings without understanding the room design.

⚠️ Réponse conceptuelle, pas une procédure de réglage.

Note: tracert = the path + per-hop latency. Locates WHERE it breaks (gateway = local, further = upstream). Compare a good trace with a bad one for intermittent issues.
