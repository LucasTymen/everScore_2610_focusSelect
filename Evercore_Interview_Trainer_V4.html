"A VLAN divides a network into separate logical networks."

KEYWORDS: VLAN, segmentation, wrong port, escalate.

Q6.9: A laptop cannot access the network. What do you do? Essential

KEYWORDS: ipconfig /all, ping, nslookup, Test-NetConnection, one layer at a time.

KEYWORDS: nslookup internal vs public, infrastructure, revert temp fixes.

KEYWORDS: ipconfig, ping, tracert, nslookup, Test-NetConnection, gpresult.
